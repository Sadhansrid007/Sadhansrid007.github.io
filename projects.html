04:12 — flagship

Video Intelligence & RAG Pipeline

CDSAML internship

A video-to-video intelligence system: ingest raw footage, auto-generate highlights, and let a user query the video in natural language to pull the exact clip they mean. I own the chatbot / Q&A layer — the part that turns "show me the moment X happened" into a retrieved clip.

  • Built the natural-language Q&A component over video content using LangChain + Llama 3.2
  • Whisper for transcription; HuggingFace embeddings for retrieval
  • MoviePy for programmatic clip generation from retrieved timestamps
  • Shipped as an interactive Streamlit UI, with python-dotenv for key management
